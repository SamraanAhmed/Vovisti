FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Everything You Need To Know

How does the Dual-Domain Switcher work for website visitors?
The switcher seamlessly toggles site content, navigation, and featured services between Study Abroad and Travel Visas with a single click, ensuring visitors immediately find relevant information without distraction.
Can clients book directly via Google Calendar or Calendly?
Yes! The platform integrates direct API calendar sync, letting clients view available counselor slots in real time and receive automated instant email and WhatsApp reminders.
Is the site fully optimized for mobile devices?
Absolutely. The website uses fluid glassmorphic breakpoints, fast-loading optimized media, and touch-friendly controls tested across iOS and Android viewports.
